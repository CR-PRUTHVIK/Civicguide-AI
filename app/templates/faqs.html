Anti-Fraud Warning: How to Spot Fake Government Portals

Fraudulent websites often mimic government portals to charge exorbitant fees or steal personal Aadhaar and banking data.

1. Check Domain Suffix All official Indian Government portals strictly end in .gov.in or .nic.in. Never trust .com / .org / .online portals.
2. No Bribes or Middlemen Government statutory fees for caste, domicile, or voter services are nominal (₹15 to ₹50 or FREE on ECI). No agent can speed up statutory field inquiries.
3. Verify QR Codes Legitimate digital certificates issued via e-District have a scannable cryptographic QR code that opens the official state government verification link.

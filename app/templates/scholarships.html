NSP OTR via Aadhaar Face Authentication

One Time Registration (OTR) is mandatory. Download the official NSP OTR App and AadhaarFaceRd App from Google Play Store to complete face authentication and obtain your permanent 14-digit OTR number.

Aadhaar-NPCI Bank Account Mapping

Scholarship funds are disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Ensure your active bank account is seeded with Aadhaar in the NPCI mapper. Do not use inactive or minor accounts without full KYC.

Top Reasons for Scholarship Application Rejection & How to Avoid Them

1. Bank Account Not NPCI Mapped

Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account via NPCI Mapper. If you have multiple accounts, specify the primary active DBT account at your bank branch.

2. Name/DOB Mismatch

Ensure the spelling of your name, father's name, and date of birth in your Aadhaar card exactly matches your Class 10 Passing Certificate and Bank Passbook.

3. Incomplete Institute Verification

After online submission, immediately submit the physical copy with fees receipts to your College/Institute Nodal Officer (INO) for level-1 approval before the cut-off date.

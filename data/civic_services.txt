Civic Services Information

Caste Certificate

A caste certificate is an official document that provides information about a person's caste or community.

Eligibility:
A person may apply for a caste certificate if they belong to a community for which such certification is applicable under the relevant government rules.

Required Documents:
Applicants may need identity proof, address proof, and supporting documents related to caste or community.

Application:
The applicant should submit the application through the appropriate government service channel and provide the required information and documents.

Income Certificate

An income certificate is an official document that records the income of an individual or family.

Required Documents:
Applicants may need identity proof, address proof, and documents supporting the declared income.

Application:
The applicant should submit the application through the appropriate government service channel.

Scholarship

Government scholarships may provide financial assistance to eligible students.

Eligibility:
Eligibility requirements can depend on factors such as educational status, family income, category, and the specific scholarship scheme.

Required Documents:
Applicants may be required to provide identity documents, educational records, income-related documents, and other scheme-specific documents.

================================================================================
STATE POLICE & STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (PSC) EXAMINATIONS IN INDIA
================================================================================

OVERVIEW:
State governments conduct recruitment for law enforcement (State Police Forces) and state administrative civil services (State PSCs) to govern district administration, revenue collection, policing, and municipal welfare.

================================================================================
1. STATE POLICE SUB-INSPECTOR (SI) & CONSTABLE RECRUITMENT

OVERVIEW:
Every state has its dedicated Police Recruitment Board (e.g., Karnataka State Police - KSP, Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board - UPPRPB, Tamil Nadu Uniformed Services Recruitment Board - TNUSRB, Maharashtra Police, Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission - BPSSC, Rajasthan Police).

1. POLICE CONSTABLE RECRUITMENT:
   - Cadres: Civil Police Constable, Armed Reserve Constable (CAR/DAR/KSRP/PAC/BMP), Traffic Police Constable, Wireless Operator.
   - Eligibility:
     * Educational Qualification: Passed 10+2 / PUC / Intermediate / 12th standard from a recognized board (10th standard for certain tribal or home guard reserves).
     * Age Limit: 18 to 25 years for General category (relaxable up to 28-30 years for OBC/SC/ST categories as per state rules).
   - Selection Process:
     * Written Examination (Objective OMR/CBT): General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Reasoning, Basic Mathematics, State History/Geography, and Local State Language Proficiency.
     * Physical Standard Test (PST): Minimum height requirements (typically 167-168 cm for males, 152-157 cm for females; chest expansion 81-86 cm for males).
     * Physical Efficiency Test (PET): 1600 meters run or 5 km run, Long Jump, High Jump, and Shot Put.
     * Medical Examination and Police Character & Antecedent Verification.

2. POLICE SUB-INSPECTOR (SI) RECRUITMENT:
   - Roles: Station House Officer (SHO), Sub-Inspector (Civil), Sub-Inspector (Intelligence/CID), Sub-Inspector (Armed Reserve / KSRP / Armed Battalion).
   - Eligibility:
     * Educational Qualification: Any Graduate Degree (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.E./B.Tech, BBA, LLB) from a recognized university.
     * Age Limit: 21 to 28/30 years for General category (up to 33-35 years for OBC/SC/ST/In-Service candidates).
   - Selection Process:
     * Paper 1 (Descriptive / Language): Essay writing, Précis writing, and Translation between English and Local State Language (e.g., Kannada/Hindi/Tamil/Marathi).
     * Paper 2 (Objective / General Studies): Indian Constitution, Criminal Law basics (IPC/CrPC/Evidence Act awareness), Indian History, Geography, General Science, Mental Ability, Ethics, and Current Affairs.
     * Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Endurance Test (ET/PET):
       - Male Candidates: 1600m run (within 6 min 30 sec - 7 min), Long Jump (3.80m), High Jump (1.20m), Shot Put (7.26 kg for 5.60m).
       - Female Candidates: 400m run (within 2 minutes), Long Jump (2.50m), Shot Put (4 kg for 3.75m).
     * Viva-Voce / Interview (in certain states) and Medical Test.

2. STATE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSIONS (STATE CIVIL SERVICES / COMBINED STATE SERVICES)

OVERVIEW:
State Public Service Commissions (State PSCs) conduct annual or biennial combined competitive examinations to recruit administrative leaders for the state government machinery.

TOP STATE PSCs & EXAMS:
- Karnataka: KPSC - Karnataka Administrative Services (KAS) Gazetted Probationers Exam.
- Uttar Pradesh: UPPSC - Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services (PCS) Exam.
- Maharashtra: MPSC - State Services Examination (Rajyaseva).
- Bihar: BPSC - Combined Competitive Examination (CCE).
- Tamil Nadu: TNPSC - Group 1 & Group 2 Services Examination.
- West Bengal: WBPSC - West Bengal Civil Service (Executive) Exam (WBCS).
- Rajasthan: RPSC - Rajasthan Administrative Services (RAS) / RTS Exam.
- Andhra Pradesh & Telangana: APPSC & TSPSC Group-I Services.
- Madhya Pradesh: MPPSC - State Service Examination.

PRESTIGIOUS POSTS OFFERED:
1. Deputy Collector / Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) - Group A
2. Deputy Superintendent of Police (DySP) - Group A
3. Assistant Commissioner of Commercial Taxes (ACCT) - Group A
4. Assistant Registrar of Co-operative Societies
5. Tehsildar / Taluk Executive Magistrate - Group B
6. Block Development Officer (BDO) / Assistant Director of Panchayats
7. District Treasury Officer (DTO) / District Welfare Officer

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Nationality: Citizen of India.
- Domicile & Language Requirement: Candidate must have adequate knowledge of the official state language (reading, writing, speaking). Some states require state domicile or passing local language in Class 10.
- Educational Qualification: Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized University.
- Age Limits:
  * Minimum Age: 21 years.
  * Maximum Age: 35 to 40/42 years depending on the state (e.g. 35 for KPSC General, 40 for UPPSC/BPSC, 38 for MPSC, with relaxations for reserved categories).

EXAMINATION ARCHITECTURE (3-TIER SYSTEM):
1. PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION (SCREENING):
   - Paper I: General Studies (History, Geography, Polity, Economy, State-specific Culture & Heritage, Environment).
   - Paper II: General Mental Ability / CSAT (Logical Reasoning, Data Interpretation, Comprehension, Decision Making).
   - Negative marking: 1/3rd or 1/4th mark deduction. Qualifying for Mains.

2. MAINS EXAMINATION (DESCRIPTIVE ESSAY & WRITTEN):
   - Compulsory Language Papers: English and Local State Language (Qualifying).
   - Essay Paper: Socio-economic, philosophical, environmental, and administrative topics.
   - General Studies Papers (GS-I to GS-VI):
     * Indian and State History, Art & Culture.
     * Constitution, Polity, Governance, and State Administrative Structure.
     * Economy, Agriculture, Science, Technology, and Rural Development.
     * Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude, and Public Administration Case Studies.
     * In-depth State Geography, State Budgets, Demographic profiles, and State Welfare Schemes.

3. PERSONALITY TEST (INTERVIEW):
   - Tests leadership, communication in state administrative language, awareness of local socio-political issues, decision-making under crisis, and ethical aptitude.
   - Final merit rank is prepared on the basis of Mains Written Marks + Interview Marks.
